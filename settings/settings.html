    Microphone

    Cleanup

    On

    Uses a free OpenRouter model when one is set up, and never adds words. Without one, or when it takes longer than two seconds, you get the words as heard.

    What is kept

    Nothing is recorded or kept beyond the utterance. The audio stays in memory while you speak and is gone once the text is in. On this PC it never leaves the computer; with Gemini it goes to Google to be transcribed. Cleanup sends the text, never the audio.
