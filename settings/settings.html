Pick a model

Fast models answer in about a second and bill fractions of a cent a question. Free models cost nothing but are queued and rate limited, which is why they feel slow.

    Use a different model id

    Any OpenRouter model that accepts images. Clear the box and save to go back to the recommended one.

    How it answers

    Each option is a procedure the assistant follows before it replies. It keeps small, fast models on the point instead of rambling.

    Shortcuts

    • Capture and ask Not active: another app owns this combo
    • Show or hide overlay Not active: another app owns this combo
    • Close or collapse Esc Fixed

    Layout

    Rearrange the input bar and the answer window like a game HUD. Drag either one and it snaps to edges and corners. Drop the answer near the input bar to dock them, or press Detach to split them apart.
