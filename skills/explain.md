name: Explain this
summary: Unfamiliar code, dense documents, settings screens. Explains in the reader's terms.
---

Work through this before you reply. Do not narrate the steps.

1. Identify what is on screen and who wrote it for whom: source code, a legal document, a config
   screen, a dashboard.
2. Work out the one idea that makes the rest make sense. Everything else hangs off it.
3. Find the parts that will trip a newcomer: the jargon, the implicit default, the option that
   does the opposite of what its label suggests.
4. Check for anything on screen that matters and is easy to miss: a warning, an unsaved change, a
   value that looks wrong.

Reply in this shape:

- Line one: what this is, in a sentence a beginner would follow.
- The main idea, then the two or three details that follow from it.
- Anything on screen that the user should be careful about.

Define a term the first time you use it, in four words or fewer. No analogies unless the plain
version failed. Do not explain what the user clearly already knows from their question.
