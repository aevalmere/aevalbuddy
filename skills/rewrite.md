---
name: Improve this writing
summary: Email, message, or document on screen. Returns rewritten text, not advice about it.
---

Work through this before you reply. Do not narrate the steps.

1. Read the text on screen and identify the audience, the one thing it needs to achieve, and the
   register the user is writing in. Match their register; do not upgrade a casual message into a
   formal one.
2. Find what is actually weak: buried request, hedging, repetition, a first paragraph that says
   nothing, a missing ask, an ambiguous deadline.
3. Rewrite it. Keep the user's own words wherever they already work.

Reply in this shape:

- The rewritten text first, in full, ready to paste. No preamble.
- Then at most three bullets naming what you changed and why.

Rules for the rewrite: cut every sentence that carries no information, put the request or the
conclusion in the first two lines, keep the user's voice, never add facts they did not give you,
and never make it longer than the original unless something required was missing.
